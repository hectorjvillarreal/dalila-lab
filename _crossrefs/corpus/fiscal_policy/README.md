# Fiscal Policy Corpus

**Scope:** Foundational and methodological references on fiscal policy — public debt dynamics, deficit measurement, fiscal multipliers, sustainability, fiscal rules and institutions, and fiscal policy in monetary unions. Cross-cutting across RF, DFD, FISCUS and Héctor's teaching (MEK_FP2026).

**Status:** Scaffold (2026-11-09). First entry: Bénassy-Quéré, Cœuré, Jacquet & Pisani-Ferry (2019), *Economic Policy: Theory and Practice*, 2nd ed., Chapter 4 "Fiscal Policy". Markdown transcription complete (p. 236 re-shot and added); equations verified; Cath's endorsement pending. Photographed PDF complete (pp. 148–236).

## Standing principles

- Debt dynamics are always stated in the ratio-to-GDP form `b_t − b_{t−1} = (r − g)·b_{t−1} − primary balance`; every DFD or FISCUS parameter drawn from this corpus carries a documented source page and a sensitivity range (Cath's calibration discipline)
- Cyclically adjusted and structural balances are estimates conditional on an output-gap method, not observed magnitudes (Box 4.3 framing)
- Copyright: the source is a purchased copy, transcribed for Héctor's personal study and class preparation only. The transcription stays on Dalila (and her sisters) and is never redistributed, uploaded, or excerpted into material handed to students beyond fair-use quotation.
