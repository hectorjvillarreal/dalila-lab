# Debt-stabilising primary balance under r < g

**Thread.** Bénassy-Quéré et al. (2019) Boxes 4.5 and 4.13 give the debt-stabilising primary balance $pb^* = \frac{r-g}{1+g} d$ and the intertemporal budget constraint in present-value form. Both presume $r > g$. When $r \le g$ the discount factor no longer discounts: $\tau^*$ and the present-value constraint lose their usual meaning, and the "stabilising" primary balance becomes a deficit. The book notes the case (pp. 164–165, 194–195) but does not develop it.

**Why it is load-bearing.** The DFD government budget constraint and RF's fiscal-rule framing (Option A debt paths) both lean on $pb^*$. Mexico's $r - g$ has changed sign across the 2000–2025 sample, so the regime question is empirical, not academic.

**Open questions.** (1) Which $r$: effective nominal rate on SHRFSP, or marginal rate? (2) How to state sustainability when $r < g$ is persistent but not permanent (Blanchard 2019; Mehrotra & Sergeyev 2021). (3) Treatment of valuation effects on foreign-currency debt in the regime test.
