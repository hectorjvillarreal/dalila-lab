# Anne — UNFPA Note: Endorsement + Adjudication

## 1. Endorsement

Endorsed. The note is correct on the load-bearing points: report-not-revision discriminator;
non-representative convenience sample treated as directional signal, never prevalence;
framing-layer-only DFD relevance behind the identification wall; verdict untouched.

**One relabel (binding).** Retag the indicator from `tfr (reproductive aspirations)` to
`desired_fertility (aspirational, not realized TFR)`. Stated ideal family size and a realized
period rate are different quantities and must not be conflatable in corpus search — the
baseline-TFR episode is the reason this is non-negotiable. Frontmatter otherwise stands.

## 2. Adjudication — agency-constraint vs SDT value-change

**Verdict: complementary, at different levels. Not competing.**

- UNFPA measures **stated aspirations + perceived barriers**; DFD 2b/2c measures **realized
  partnership behavior** (union composition). Different objects.
- The persistence of a two-child ideal alongside a collapse in realized partnership is the
  ordinary **aspiration-behavior gap** — consistent with, not contradictory to, a compositional
  collapse. SDT never required that people stop *wanting* marriage/children; it is a claim about
  de-institutionalization of marriage and life-course reordering *in practice*.
- **Potentially unifying:** the economic/housing constraints UNFPA names are candidate
  **proximate mechanisms** for the extensive-margin retreat we found (can't afford a household →
  union doesn't form → no childbearing union). Constraints may *drive* channel-assisted
  compression rather than compete with it.

**Guardrail (append to the note):** the "desire persists" finding is **not** evidence against
cohort-replacement and must not be cited as such. Aspiration ≠ realized behavior. Preempt the
argument that persistent ideals refute the SDT/cohort account.

**Residual tension (not settled here):** reversibility. UNFPA implies constraint-relief →
rebound toward desired ~2; SDT/cohort-replacement implies a sticky regime shift (European SDT did
not reverse on economic improvement). Our data cannot settle this; it is downstream of the paper.
→ watch item, §3.

## 3. Watch item — opened, narrow

**Scope:** evidence on **fertility reversibility** — whether pro-natal / housing / childcare
policy actually closes the desired-achieved gap. **Not** the framing debate (settled as
complementary above).

**Rationale (scenario, not curiosity):** if the unmet-desire, policy-tractable reading has
weight, the DFD **fast-transition central path carries a latent upside risk** it does not
currently represent. That is a scenario-discipline question, and it also feeds Aurora's long-run
demographic-transmission assumptions.
