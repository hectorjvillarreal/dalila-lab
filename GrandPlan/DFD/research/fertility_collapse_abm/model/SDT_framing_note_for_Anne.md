# Framing Note: SDT as the Theoretical Home

## 1. The gap

Stage 2b gave a positioned negative result: the rapid collapse is **not** a within-cohort reflexive
cascade; it is **cohort-replacement** (marriage abandoned for cohabitation and singlehood, extensive
margin) with, in COL, a secondary exogenous-period overlay. That finding has no named theoretical
home in the corpus. The Second Demographic Transition is the candidate, and its absence would be
conspicuous to any demography referee.

## 2. What SDT is, briefly

Lesthaeghe & van de Kaa (1986); van de Kaa (1987); Lesthaeghe (2010, PDR). A regime shift in family
formation: retreat from marriage, rise of cohabitation, postponement of union and childbearing,
sub-replacement fertility, de-linking of sex, partnership, and parenthood — driven by value change
toward autonomy and self-realization. Its empirical core **is** the union-composition shift Stage 2b
measures.

## 3. Why it fits — lead with the convergence

The mapping is direct: SDT *is* the marriage→cohabitation/singlehood, extensive-margin shift. But the
part to **lead with** is the convergence. SDT is fundamentally a **cohort-diffusion** theory —
successive cohorts adopting new family behaviors. Stage 2b's verdict is **cohort-dominant**. The
theory and the identification are the same claim reached two ways. That is corroboration, not
coincidence, and it is the strongest reason to adopt the frame.

## 4. The LAC structure — caveat *and* contribution

LAC is **not** a clean SDT case. It carries a centuries-old **consensual-union tradition** (unión
libre), historically concentrated in lower-SES, indigenous, and Afro-descendant populations,
predating any SDT. Esteve, Lesthaeghe & López-Gay (2012, *The Latin American Cohabitation Boom,
1970–2007*, PDR) show the recent rise as the **modern, SDT-style face diffusing up the social ladder
over that old base** — the "two faces" of LAC cohabitation.

This is promoted from caveat to candidate **contribution**. The two-faces structure may be **why** the
collapse is so fast here. Europe had to build cohabitation as a new institution from near-zero; LAC
already had the scaffolding, so the modern retreat from marriage propagates through an **existing
channel with far less friction**. That is a speed mechanism European SDT never needed. It also yields a
**falsifiable prediction**: where the consensual-union channel is absent — marriage-dominant ARG/CHL —
the collapse should be slower or differently structured. The external-validity test is no longer a
nuisance; it is the discriminating prediction of the frame.

## 5. What SDT does not explain — *channel-assisted* compression

SDT gives direction and composition, not **tempo**. European SDT unfolded over ~40–50 years; the LAC
collapse is single-digit years. The claim is therefore not "compressed SDT" — compression is not the
whole story — but **channel-assisted compression**: compression *enabled by* the pre-existing
consensual-union channel. With JFV's overshoot (middle-income TFR below rich) as the twist SDT never
anticipated, the frame names the phenomenon; the channel-assisted speed and the overshoot are ours.

## 6. Positioning — resolved

- **(a) Frame, not causal claim.** We cannot test value change and will not pretend to. SDT's status
  as descriptive rather than predictive (Coleman; Cliquet) suits a measurement-and-discrimination
  paper.
- **(b) Primary LAC strand: Esteve, Lesthaeghe & López-Gay** on the cohabitation boom.
- **(c) Load-bearing in the framing, frame-only in the inference.** The one place the frame touches
  inference is the ARG/CHL channel-absent prediction (§4) — a testable implication, not a value-change
  assertion.
