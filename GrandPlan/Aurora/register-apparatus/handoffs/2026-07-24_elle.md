## 1. What was built

**`GrandPlan/Aurora/auto-apparatus/`** — a read-only obligation scanner over
the Aurora registers (AI, briefing, geopol, docs/corpus) and
`_crossrefs/design_log/`. One command renders `board.md`: pending approvals
and endorsements, corroboration-pending claims, dangling cross-refs, version
hygiene, unprocessed sources, live triggers, declared actions, estimate-decay
register. `run_log.md` accumulates one row per scan.

Disciplines, enforced by construction: the corpus documents are the only
source of truth (the tool holds no state); no judgment is fabricated (whether
a trigger fired is decided in the documents, by their owners); endorsement
and approval fields are never touched by a maintenance run. It is the
minimal answer to the design question you saw logged in the Mollick entry —
scoped to the apparatus, not to research judgment.
