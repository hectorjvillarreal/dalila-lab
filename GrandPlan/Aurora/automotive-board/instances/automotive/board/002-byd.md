# Entry 002 — BYD  ·  *pure diffusion integrator (board's volume keystone)*

**Structure:** BYD Company (HKEX 1211 / SZSE 002594). A battery and electronics firm that makes cars: in-house cells (Blade), power electronics, semis, supply chain. Brands laddered upward: BYD (Dynasty/Ocean), Denza, Fang Cheng Bao, Yangwang.
**Distinctive asset — deepest vertical integration in the industry.** Owns the cost-determining layers, so it compresses unit cost as assembly-first rivals cannot. The diffusion weapon is *owning the stack*, not buying brands.
**The bet:** the diffusion machine compounds — cost compression + scale + integration build a cost position no one matches, and brand/premium/global access *follow from* cost dominance. *(Counter: Geely.)*
**Two-level shakeout:** organic premium climb (Denza→Yangwang) is the inverse of Geely buying premium — the wager is directly observable.
**Watch items:** *(Gina)* local-plant tariff defense (Hungary, Brazil…) — does building inside the walls neutralize them? · margin under price-war pressure · organic premium traction · export concentration.

The "brand follows cost" half is not visible; the organic premium climb has no confirming data.
**Critical reading — archetype ≠ instantiation.** **Leapmotor** was the *only* top-ten grower in H1-2026: ~90k monthly retail (June), **>65% of core components in-house**, targeting the ¥60,000–200,000 band amid "consumption downgrading," exports +372.6% (96,300). That is BYD's archetype — vertical integration, cost compression, volume — executed *better*, by a later and smaller player, down-market. So the correct reading is **not** "diffusion is losing"; it is "**the diffusion archetype is fine; BYD's instantiation is failing.**" A ranked board would have drawn the opposite conclusion. *First time the archetype-not-ranking rule produced a materially different answer.* *(To verify: Leapmotor is believed part-owned by Stellantis with an export JV — which would make it the **inverse of the VW–XPeng reversal**: a Western incumbent buying into a Chinese challenger for *channel* rather than *stack*.)*
