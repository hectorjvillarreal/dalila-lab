# Entry 007 — Hyundai Motor Group  ·  *not a corner — the board's first genuine mixture. Control case for the incumbent axis.*

**Why it earned entry — basis gap, not roster.** Both prior incumbents (Toyota, VW) were incumbents *in distress*, perfectly collinear: the board could not distinguish "incumbent" from "incumbent losing." Hyundai is a non-Chinese full-line incumbent that is **competitive**. It separates the axis — and then does better, by *decomposing* it.

**Structure:** Hyundai Motor Co. + Kia + Genesis under Hyundai Motor Group; chaebol vertical chain — Mobis (components), Glovis (logistics), steel. ~80% of **Boston Dynamics**. Motional (L4 JV). 42dot (SDV subsidiary, CEO Minwoo Park — ex-Nvidia VP autonomous driving, Tesla's first computer vision engineer). Executive Chair Chung Euisun.

**The bet (falsifiable) — the un-cornered middle.** The board's implicit thesis is that **pure poles win** (BYD's cost, Tesla's autonomy). Hyundai wagers the opposite: the winning position is a legally-permitted combination of everything, cash-funded by a healthy auto business, converting into physical AI *before* the auto business decays. *Kill condition: if archetype purity wins, the middle is squeezed from both ends.*

**The China lesson (bears directly on 005):** Hyundai sold ~42,900 units in China in Q3 2025 — it has **already lost China, essentially completely, and is fine**, because China was never its profit engine and it replaced it with the US, India, emerging markets. *Losing China is survivable if you diversified first.* VW's problem is not that it is losing China; it is that it had nothing else.
