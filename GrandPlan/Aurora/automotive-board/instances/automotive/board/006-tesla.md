# Entry 006 — Tesla  ·  *frontier disruptor (board's origin point)*

**Why it anchors:** every other entry is partly defined against it. The frontier pole that makes "diffusion vs frontier" legible; the original compute-first car (003 runs a play Tesla wrote); the *young* incumbent against Toyota's and VW's old ones.
**The bet (purest frontier wager):** the car is a software/autonomy/energy platform, not a manufacturing-cost game; the prize is autonomy + the data-compute flywheel, plus optionality outside the car (energy storage, robotics).
**The disrupted disruptor:** the company that did to Detroit what BYD now does to everyone is itself being out-diffused on cost and volume — **BYD (2.26M BEV) overtook Tesla (1.64M, −9%) as the world's largest EV maker in 2025**, Tesla's second straight annual decline and its biggest drop ever. The escape isn't to win the car-cost war it's losing — it's to *change the battlefield* onto autonomy/robotics, where compute-and-data moats count and diffusion economics don't reach.
**Watch items:** robotaxi scale-up (unsupervised in Austin from Jan 2026; Dallas/Houston added; fleet still small, behind Waymo; Cybercab production ~Apr 2026) · Optimus production (~2026) · the "brand follows cost" half failing — does autonomy arrive before volume erosion bites? · *(Gina)* Musk's political entanglement, brand polarization, and Tesla's deep China exposure while competing with China.
