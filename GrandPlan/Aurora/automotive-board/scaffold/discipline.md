# Discipline — invariant

*Portable across industries. Nothing in this file may name a player, a technology, a
country, or any other instance-specific object. If a sentence here would be false for a
different industry's board, it belongs in that instance's `findings.md`, not here.*

Instances state each principle concretely in their own `instances/<industry>/findings.md`,
under headings that cross-reference the anchors below.

---

## Archetype, not ranking {#archetype-not-ranking}

Players are entered **by strategy archetype, not by rank.** "Who becomes top player" is
not one race; it is a question of which archetype the environment ends up rewarding. The
board tracks the *tournament*, not a leaderboard. No tool may rank or order players by
any metric.

## Poles unbundle under data — and that is the instrument refining, not failing {#poles-unbundle}

Poles chosen intuitively tend to be composites mistaken for extremes; contact with data
separates their components. This is the archetypal-analysis purity problem arriving
empirically, and it settles the quantification question: **loadings cannot be computed on
a basis whose elements keep splitting.** The qualitative discipline is not a compromise
pending better method; it is the only representation that survives contact. When a pole
cracks, split it by hand — never patch it by computation.

## Entry is by basis gap {#entry-by-basis-gap}

A player earns entry when the board cannot distinguish two things it needs to
distinguish — a gap in the classification basis — never to complete a roster or reflect
market size. Adding, merging, or retiring a player is a human act.

## Thresholds carry dates {#thresholds-carry-dates}

Every falsifiable bet names the observations that confirm or kill it, and every such
observation that can be booked gets a dated threshold. Date checks are mechanical
(tool-run); resolution is judgment (human-set). A tool never resolves a threshold.

## Single-exemplar poles inherit their setter's incidental correlations {#single-exemplar-poles}

A pole defined by one exemplar bundles that exemplar's accidental properties into the
archetype. Pair-completion — a second exemplar per pole — is the corrective; poles with
two exemplars are the ones that resist cracking.

## Archetype ≠ instantiation {#archetype-not-instantiation}

A pole-setter underperforming does not falsify its archetype: a later player may run the
same archetype better. A ranked board would collapse this distinction and draw the
opposite conclusion; an archetype board keeps it visible.

## The facts/judgment line {#facts-judgment-line}

> Facts are mechanical and basis-independent → automate.
> Classification is judgment and basis-dependent → never automate.

Every entry is a prose file (human-owned; archetype, loadings, the bet, readings, watch
items, status) plus a facts file (tool-writable slot values only). The line is enforced
structurally: the tool has nowhere to write judgment.

# Portable findings — abstract forms

*Each was first found in one instance; the concrete statement lives in that instance's
`findings.md` under the matching anchor. Recorded here in industry-free form because the
form travels.*

## The synthetic chokepoint {#synthetic-chokepoint}

Diffusion industries have no natural chokepoint to sanction. Where none exists, a
regulator can **manufacture one** — a compliance gate framed on an adjacent concern that
functions as a market-access chokepoint. Frontier/chokepoint logic can be imported into a
diffusion industry by regulatory construction, and it works. *For any new instance, ask:
what is the synthetic chokepoint here?*

## Constraints can be legislated into existence {#legislated-constraints}

An apparent structural law of the strategy space may in fact be a **policy instrument —
discretionary, selectively applied, and revocable.** Test any "forbidden region" against
a player who sits inside it unharmed; if one exists, the constraint is gated on something
the space itself does not encode (e.g. origin of capital), and it is a foresight object
in its own right.

## Walls channel; they do not stop {#wall-gradient}

Excluding a player from one market redirects it to others rather than halting it. Wall
height forms a gradient: fully walled markets exclude, partially walled markets slow,
unwalled markets flip. **Unwalled markets are the preview** of what walled markets are
walling out.

## Shakeouts have institutional forms {#shakeout-institutional-forms}

The same competitive pressure resolves through different national mechanisms — e.g.
elimination through open tournament, negotiated consolidation through an association,
restructuring blocked by governance rules. Institutions constrain the *shape* a strategy
can take, and negotiated forms are slow by construction because consent is their price.
Consolidation may also proceed **along national lines**: domestic ties tightening while
cross-border alliances loosen, re-forming the competitive unit around the nation-state.

## Regulation reshapes the tournament from both ends {#regulation-both-ends}

Regulators can simultaneously exclude an ecosystem's *strong* players abroad and squeeze
its *weak* players at home; both movements favour the large and integrated.
Consolidation by regulation, symmetric.

## The level limitation {#level-limitation}

A firm-level board may mis-locate the contest: the decisive advantage may be an
ecosystem or national-system property, not a firm property. This is **not fixed by adding
a player** — a coordination level is a *level*, not an archetype, and must not be entered
as a pole. The candidate corrective is a separate **coalition register** alongside the
firm-level board, adopted only when the instance demands it.
