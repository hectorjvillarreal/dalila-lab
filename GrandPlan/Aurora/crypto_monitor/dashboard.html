1 Runs UNSET

A coordination failure in which holders rush to exit because they expect others to exit, making the exit self-fulfilling. Applies to any claim redeemable or sellable faster than the underlying can absorb.

Three run objects: spot-ETF holders (IBIT, ETHA and peers) whose redemptions hit spot; stablecoin holders (USDT, USDC) whose redemptions force the issuer to sell reserves; exchange depositors.

2 Fire Sales / Leverage UNSET
