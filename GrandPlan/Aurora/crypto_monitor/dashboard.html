1 Runs AMBER

A coordination failure in which holders rush to exit because they expect others to exit, making the exit self-fulfilling. Applies to any claim redeemable or sellable faster than the underlying can absorb.

2 Fire Sales / Leverage AMBER

Asset-price declines force deleveraging by holders who financed positions with debt or hold obligations collateralized by the asset; forced selling depresses prices further, in a reflexive loop.

Perpetual futures liquidate automatically at known margins; treasury companies hold coins financed with convertibles or preferred stock; miners carry debt against hashprice; ETH liquid-staking tokens can trade at a discount that forces levered unwinds.
