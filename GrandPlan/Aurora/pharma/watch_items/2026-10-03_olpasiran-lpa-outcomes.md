# Olpasiran outcomes — does deeper Lp(a) lowering succeed where pelacarsen failed?

## Origin

Opened by Héctor on 2026-10-03 after the ad hoc Amgen (`AMGN`, roster #3) news
sweep. The trigger was the 2026-09-04 Novartis disclosure that pelacarsen's
Lp(a)HORIZON outcomes trial missed its primary endpoint (feed item
20261003-0007, rejected as an echo; the event itself stands).

## The thread

Lp(a)HORIZON (pelacarsen, ~8,300 patients, ~6 years) lowered Lp(a) but did not
significantly reduce the composite of CV death, MI, stroke and urgent coronary
revascularization. This is the first outcomes test of the Lp(a) hypothesis,
and it failed. Amgen's olpasiran is the next test:

- **OCEAN(a)-Outcomes** (NCT05581303): 7,297 patients with established ASCVD
  and elevated Lp(a), olpasiran every 12 weeks vs placebo. Primary endpoint:
  time to CHD death, MI or urgent coronary revascularization (no stroke,
  unlike HORIZON's composite). Status ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING; **estimated
  primary completion 2028-03-31** (registry last updated 2026-02-27).
- **OCEAN(a)-PreEvent** (NCT07136012): primary-prevention trial, later.
- Olpasiran lowers Lp(a) more than pelacarsen (>95% vs ~70–80% in Phase 2),
  which is the "dose" argument for a different result.

## Modeling implication

For the pharma board, not DFD. The `AMGN` 2028 LOE cluster (Otezla, Repatha,
Enbrel) needs replacement revenue; olpasiran is one of the large-market
cardiovascular bets alongside MariTide. A HORIZON-style miss removes a
replacement candidate in the same window as the 2028 cliff. A win would be
the first proof that lowering Lp(a) reduces events, a new drug class with a
large eligible population and payer exposure. In both cases the pelacarsen
result changes the prior.

## Open questions

- Was the HORIZON miss about the size of the Lp(a) reduction, the endpoint
  (stroke inclusion), the population, or the hypothesis itself? Look for
  Novartis's full presentation and subgroup data (dose-response by achieved
  Lp(a)).
- Does Amgen revise OCEAN(a)-Outcomes timing, sample size or endpoints after
  HORIZON? Any interim or event-driven early readout?
- How do the other Lp(a) programs respond? Not verified in this pass; check
  before citing.

## Status

Watch item, not active commitment. Cadence: low. Check at each Amgen
quarterly release and on any ClinicalTrials.gov update to NCT05581303.

## Triggers for promotion

1. Any OCEAN(a)-Outcomes readout (topline, interim or futility stop).
2. Amgen changes the trial's design, endpoints or completion date by a year
   or more.
3. Published HORIZON analyses that attribute the miss to insufficient Lp(a)
   reduction (this bears directly on olpasiran's chances).
